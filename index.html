📖 NFL Blowout Pick'em Rules

1. Blowout Winner Pick

Pick 1 team per week that will win by the largest margin. You get points equal to the Margin of Victory (Winner Score - Loser Score). If they lose, you get 0 points.

2. Blowout Loser Pick

Pick 1 team per week that will get blown out. You get points equal to the Margin of Defeat (Opponent Score - Your Team Score). If they win, you get 0 points.

3. Strict Single-Use Survivor Rule

You can ONLY select each NFL team ONCE per season, regardless of whether you pick them as a Winner or a Loser. Once used in any week, they are locked/burnt for the rest of the year!

4. Season Champion

The player with the highest total points at the end of Week 18 wins the championship!
