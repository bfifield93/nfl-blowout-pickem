📖 NFL Blowout Pick'em Rules

1. Single Pick Per Week

Each player makes ONLY 1 pick total per week. You can choose a Blowout Winner Pick or a Blowout Loser Pick.

2. Scoring Margin Points

- Winner Pick: Earn points equal to the Margin of Victory ($Winner\ Score - Loser\ Score$) if your team wins (0 pts if they lose).
- Loser Pick: Earn points equal to the Margin of Defeat ($Opponent\ Score - Your\ Team\ Score$) if your team loses (0 pts if they win).

3. Single-Use Team Lock ("Survivor Rule")

You can ONLY select each NFL team ONCE per season (whether picked to win or lose). Once selected in any week, that team is burnt for the rest of the year!

4. Season Champion

The player with the highest total points at the end of Week 18 wins the championship!
